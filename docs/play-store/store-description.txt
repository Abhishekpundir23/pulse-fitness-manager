Run your gym’s daily records from one phone. Pulse Fitness Manager brings members, memberships, payment records, attendance and expenses into one place, with core management available offline.

MAKE IT YOUR GYM
Create your own membership plans with the names, durations in months and prices you offer. Save your gym details, add members with optional photos, or import a member list from CSV.

FIND MEMBERS AND FOLLOW UP
Search by name, phone number or member ID. Filter active, expiring, expired and cancelled memberships, or focus on paid members and outstanding dues. Sort by name, expiry date, newest members or highest dues. Review membership periods and keep older unpaid balances visible after a renewal.

KEEP PAYMENT RECORDS CLEAR
Record payments collected by cash, UPI, card or bank transfer. Search payment history by month and method, share a membership invoice as a PDF, and reverse an incorrect entry with a reason while retaining its history. These are records of payments handled outside the app; Pulse does not process payments or refunds.

MANAGE ATTENDANCE
Check members in, see who is present, and search the register. Choose an earlier date to review or correct attendance. The home screen shows today’s check-ins and the last seven days at a glance.

UNDERSTAND YOUR GYM’S CASH FLOW
Review collections and expenses by month, compare six months of collections and see the payment-method breakdown. Use the expense ledger to search every entry and review costs across one month or all time. Current unpaid dues are shown separately from monthly cash flow.

KEEP CONTROL OF YOUR DATA
Your gym records are stored in the app’s private storage on your phone. No online account or cloud sync is required. Save a manual backup to a folder you choose, review a backup before restoring it, and use a local recovery copy to undo a restore. Keep a verified backup outside the phone: uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes its local records. Exported backups are not encrypted by Pulse.

Prepare a WhatsApp renewal or dues reminder when you choose to follow up. You review and send it yourself; the app does not send messages automatically. Sharing and WhatsApp use the apps and services you select.

Built for gym owners managing one phone. There are no ads, analytics SDKs or integrated payment gateway in this version.
